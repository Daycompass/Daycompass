## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do **not** open a public GitHub issue for security
vulnerabilities.

Instead, report it privately to: `security@example.org` (placeholder -
replace with a real address).

Include:

- A description of the issue and its potential impact.
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept.
- The affected version or commit.

We aim to acknowledge reports within a reasonable timeframe and will
coordinate on a disclosure timeline with you.
